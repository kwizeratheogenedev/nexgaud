# Brief: stolen phone IMEI blocking in Rwanda

For meetings with RURA, MTN Rwanda, Airtel Rwanda and the Rwanda National Police / RIB. A starting point, not a legal document: check every point with them.

## The problem

A stolen phone keeps its value because it can be resold and used on any network. NexGuard's registry lets buyers and shops check an IMEI before buying, but only the operators can stop a stolen phone from working.

## What NexGuard already has

- A registry of devices with their IMEIs, owners and proof of ownership (box or receipt).
- Stolen reports that need a police case number and are checked by the NexGuard team before they show.
- A public check (web, USSD, SMS) and a partner API used by phone shops; police partners see the case number.
- An audit log of every report, review, dispute and change of ownership.

## What we would like to agree

1. **Which list is official.** Whether RURA keeps (or plans) a central equipment identity register (CEIR), and whether NexGuard reports can feed it, or only reports confirmed by the police.
2. **Who decides a block.** Proposed: NexGuard sends the IMEI only after the police confirm the case number; the block is RURA's or the operator's decision.
3. **How a block is lifted.** When a phone is recovered, or a report was a mistake: who asks, how fast it is lifted.
4. **Protection against abuse.** Someone could report a phone they sold. NexGuard already has a handover code for sales and a dispute process; ask what evidence RURA and the operators need.
5. **Data sharing.** Under Law No. 058/2021: the legal basis for sharing IMEIs and case numbers, what personal data is shared (as little as possible: IMEI, case number, date), and NCSA requirements.
6. **Technical link.** A secured API or a daily file, and who pays for setting it up.

## Questions for the police / RIB

- How can NexGuard confirm a case number quickly (a contact, a portal, an API)?
- Would officers use the partner portal to check phones found in operations, and see the owner's contact through it?

## What NexGuard can offer

- The registry, the check page and the partner API, free for the police and RURA.
- Monthly statistics: phones reported, checked, found, recovered.

2. What we collect, and why

DataWhy we need it
Your phone number and nameTo create your account and sign you in with an SMS code, and to send you alerts by SMS.
The protected phone's location (position, accuracy, time)To show you where your phone is and the route it took, so you can find it.
Phone details: brand, model, Android version, app versionTo show which phone is which, and to fix problems on particular phone models.
Battery level, charging state, network type (Wi-Fi or mobile)To tell you if the phone may switch off soon, and when it was last online.
Which permissions are turned onTo warn you when protection cannot work fully.
A SIM fingerprint: a one-way code made from the SIM's network and subscriptionTo alert you when someone puts a different SIM card in your phone. It does not contain your phone number, and the SIM's details cannot be read back from it.
A push token from Google FirebaseTo wake the phone when you press Ring or Lock. The push message contains no location or personal data.
Actions you send (ring, lock, message) and alertsTo deliver them to the phone and show you their result.
Technical logs (IP address, time, the page or API called)To keep the service secure and fix errors.

We do not collect your contacts, messages, photos, call history, the phone's IMEI or the SIM's serial number. We do not use your location for advertising, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Legal basis

We process this data because you ask us to provide the NexGuard service (our agreement with you) and because you consent to sharing the phone's location when you turn on protection. You can withdraw consent at any time by stopping protection in the app or removing the phone from your account.

4. Protection against misuse

NexGuard is for finding your own devices. You may only install it on a phone you own, or on a phone whose user has clearly agreed. While NexGuard shares a phone's location, a notification always shows on that phone; there is no hidden mode. If we find that NexGuard is being used to follow someone without their agreement, we will suspend the account and may report it to the authorities.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data (removing a phone in the dashboard deletes its location history straight away);
  • stop processing your data, or object to it;
  • move your data to another service.

Write to [privacy email]. We answer within [30] days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA), which supervises personal data protection in Rwanda.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in codes and phone access keys are stored only in a protected (hashed) form. Each phone uses its own access key, which stays on that phone and is encrypted there. Only a few NexGuard staff can reach the database, and team statistics show counts only, never locations or phone numbers.

10. Children

You must be 18 or older to create an account. A parent may protect a phone used by their child, and is responsible for that use.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we tell you in the app or by SMS before the change applies.
